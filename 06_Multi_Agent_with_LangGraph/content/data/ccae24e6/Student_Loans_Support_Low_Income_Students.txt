### Overview of Student Loans for Low-Income Students

In 2024, student loan options remain accessible for students from various financial backgrounds, including those from low-income families. The federal financial aid system offers several resources aimed specifically at making college education affordable for everyone, no matter their circumstances.

1. **Federal Loans**: Most federal student loans do not impose strict income limits but require information based on financial need. For example, students can access Direct Subsidized Loans designed to assist those with demonstrated financial need. Freshmen can borrow up to $5,500, with up to $3,500 available as subsidized loans for qualifying students, ensuring that financial constraints do not hinder your educational ambitions.

2. **Income-Driven Repayment Plans**: There are specific repayment plans that help manage loan payments based on income, ensuring they remain affordable even for low-income borrowers. Payments can be capped at a low percentage of your income, greatly reducing the financial strain, with options for forgiveness after a set number of years of consistent payments, typically 20 to 25 years. We believe that everyone deserves a manageable path to repayment.

3. **Grants and Scholarships**: While not loans, federal grants like the Pell Grant provide significant financial aid. For the 2024-2025 award year, eligible undergraduate students with exceptional financial need can receive up to $7,395, which does not require repayment. This is a wonderful opportunity that can help alleviate some of the financial burdens you may face.

### Path to Financial Aid

- **FAFSA (Free Application for Federal Student Aid)**: Completing the FAFSA is an essential step for all potential college students, enabling access to various federal aid programs, including loans, grants, and work-study opportunities. It's an important tool that opens doors to financial assistance.
- **Expected Family Contribution (EFC)**: Your FAFSA submission will generate an EFC that determines your financial aid eligibility. Importantly, there is no FAFSA income limit, allowing even low-income students to qualify for various funding sources, ensuring that you can access the financial support you need.
